**Data Science Job Market Analysis**  

📊 **Analyzing key factors influencing job-seeker success using Machine Learning**  

## 🚀 **Overview**  
This project explores the **job market for data science roles**, identifying the most important factors that affect hiring success. We utilize **various machine learning models** to predict job-seeker outcomes and provide actionable insights for both job applicants and companies.  


## 🔍 **Key Insights**  
✔️ **Experience, training hours, and city development index** significantly impact job-seeking success.  
✔️ **Random Forest & XGBoost** performed best in predicting job-market outcomes.  
✔️ **Deep Learning (CNN) struggled**, suggesting traditional ML models are more effective for structured job market data.  
✔️ **Companies can optimize hiring** by focusing on key experience & training factors.  
✔️ **Job seekers can enhance employability** by targeting in-demand skills.  

## 💡 **Technologies Used**
